# NovaPS5 v0.1.2

Paste this body into the GitHub release, then attach `NovaPS5-Setup-0.1.2.exe`, `latest.yml` and
`NovaPS5-Setup-0.1.2.exe.blockmap` from `release/`.

---

## What's new

- **You decide when an update installs.** NovaPS5 still checks GitHub on every launch and still
  downloads a new version quietly in the background — but it then **waits**. Nothing restarts on its
  own and nothing replaces the app behind your back, not even if you quit NovaPS5 normally.
  When you are ready, click **Restart to update** — in the header, in the update dialog, or in
  **Settings ▸ Application updates** — and NovaPS5 installs the update, restarts and shows you what
  changed.

  Previously a downloaded update installed itself four seconds after the download finished, which could
  interrupt whatever you were doing in the app.

- **Clearer update states.** A downloaded update now reads *"NovaPS5 vX.Y.Z is ready to install"* and
  the action is spelled out as **Restart to update** instead of "Restart now", so it is obvious that
  waiting is fine.

---

Requires Windows 10/11 (x64). Python and the conversion runtime are bundled; the app installs per-user
with no admin prompt.

**Updating:** NovaPS5 v0.1.0 and v0.1.1 will download this release in the background and hold it until
you press **Restart to update**. Your library and settings are untouched.
